How to Play

The timeless playground game of love and friendship.

  1. Write down two names.
  2. Cross out the letters they share.
  3. Count the total remaining letters.
  4. Use that number to count through F.L.A.M.E.S.
  5. The last remaining letter reveals the relationship!
F Friends 🤝
L Love ❤️
A Affection 😊
M Marriage 💍
E Enemies 😡
S Siblings 👫
